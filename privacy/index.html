Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026. This policy covers the Curve Studio Plotter app for iPhone and iPad and this support website.

Graphing and local storage

The app processes formulas, handwriting and drawings on your device. It keeps drawings, saved projects and preferences in local app storage. The app reads formulas from the clipboard when you use its import or paste controls. When you export a graph or share a file, the destination you choose receives that file. The app does not require a Curve Studio account or send your graphing content to the developer during normal use.

Trial and purchases

The app records your trial start on your device. The record is stored in the iOS Keychain, where it may remain after you delete and reinstall the app. Apple processes purchases and manages subscriptions. The app uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to unlock Pro and restore purchases. The developer does not receive your full payment card details from Apple. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

App updates and diagnostics

The app checks for updates through Expo. Expo may receive a randomly generated installation identifier, IP address, app and update version information, and limited startup error details when an update fails. Expo uses this information to deliver updates, measure update adoption and diagnose problems. See Expo’s data collection documentation.

Support and website hosting

If you email support, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request and investigate reported issues. We keep support emails as needed to handle the request and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process technical information, such as your IP address, to operate and secure it. The site does not use advertising or analytics scripts. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Deleting data and contacting us

You can delete saved projects in the app. Deleting the app removes its ordinary local app data. Copies you exported or backed up may remain at their destinations, and the Keychain trial record may remain on the device. If you want to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you sent to support, email cen082033@gmail.com.

简体中文

隐私政策

本政策自 2026 年 11 月 21 日起生效，适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 上的 Curve Studio Plotter 及本支持网站。

绘图与本机存储

公式、手写内容和图形由设备本地处理。绘图、保存的项目和偏好设置保存在 App 本地。使用导入或粘贴功能时，App 会读取剪贴板里的公式。导出或分享文件时，文件会发往你选择的位置。App 无需注册账号，正常使用时不会将你的绘图内容发送给开发者。

试用和购买

试用开始时间保存在设备的 iOS 钥匙串中，卸载和重装后可能仍然保留。Apple 处理付款并管理订阅。App 使用 StoreKit 提供的交易与权益信息解锁 Pro 和恢复购买。开发者不会从 Apple 获得你的完整银行卡资料。参见 Apple 隐私政策。

更新和诊断

App 通过 Expo 检查更新。Expo 可能接收随机生成的安装标识符、IP 地址、App 和更新版本信息，以及更新失败时有限的启动错误信息。这些信息用于分发更新、统计更新采用情况和排查问题。参见 Expo 数据收集说明。

技术支持与网站托管

当你发邮件联系支持时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容和你主动发送的附件，并用这些信息回复和排查问题。我们会根据处理请求及适用的记录保存要求保留邮件。本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 可能为运营和保护服务处理 IP 地址等技术信息。本网站不使用广告或分析脚本。参见 GitHub 隐私声明。

删除数据与联系方式

你可以在 App 内删除已保存的项目。删除 App 会移除其常规本地数据，已导出或备份的副本及钥匙串中的试用记录可能仍然保留。如需查询、更正或请求删除你发送给支持的信息，请联系 cen082033@gmail.com。
