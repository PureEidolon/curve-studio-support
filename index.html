Support

Get help with Curve Studio Plotter for iPhone and iPad.

Email support

Support email: cen082033@gmail.com

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in the app, select Curve Studio Pro, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account you used to buy Pro.

Free trial

The seven-day trial starts when you first open the app and includes all tools. The trial ends without charging you. Basic Cartesian graphing remains available for free.

Report a problem

Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version and the steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots are optional. Leave out passwords, payment details and any private information visible in your screenshots.

技术支持

Curve Studio Plotter 支持 iPhone 和 iPad。如需帮助，请联系 cen082033@gmail.com。

恢复购买

在 App 内打开「设置 → Curve Studio Pro → 恢复购买」，并使用购买时的 Apple 账户。

免费试用

首次打开 App 后开始 7 天全功能试用。到期不会扣费，基础直角坐标绘图仍可免费使用。

反馈问题

请附上设备型号、iOS 或 iPadOS 版本、App 版本和复现步骤。可附截图，请先遮盖个人信息。请勿发送密码或付款信息。
